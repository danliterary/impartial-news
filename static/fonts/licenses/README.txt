These typefaces are self-hosted for Impartial News. Each is licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
Office lookalikes are labelled as such in the reader settings; they are metric-compatible open fonts, not Microsoft fonts.
Do not self-host Microsoft Office fonts (Calibri, Cambria, Arial, Times New Roman, Courier New, Georgia).

Sources were copied from the @fontsource packages (jsDelivr), latin subset, regular and bold, plus italic where the file was modest.
OpenDyslexic italic was omitted because the files are large; regular and bold are included.

Licence text for each family is in this folder as <id>.txt.
